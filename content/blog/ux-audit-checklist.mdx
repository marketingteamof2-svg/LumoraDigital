If your website gets traffic but few enquiries, the problem is rarely the traffic. It's friction. Run through this checklist to find the leaks.

## Clarity

1. **Can a visitor explain what you do within five seconds?** Test it with someone outside your company.
2. **Is the headline about the customer's outcome**, not your company?
3. **Is there one obvious primary action** on every page?

## Trust

4. **Are there real proof points above the fold** — results, logos or reviews?
5. **Do case studies include numbers**, not just adjectives?
6. **Is contact information easy to find**, including a phone number or WhatsApp?

## Forms

7. **Do you ask only for what you need?** Every extra field reduces completion.
8. **Are longer forms split into steps** with a progress indicator?
9. **Are error messages specific and shown inline**, next to the field?

## Speed and mobile

10. **Does the largest content load in under 2.5 seconds** on a mid-range phone?
11. **Are tap targets at least 44px**, with enough spacing between them?
12. **Does anything jump around while loading?** Layout shift erodes trust.

## Accessibility

13. **Does text meet WCAG AA contrast** (4.5:1 for body text)?
14. **Can you navigate the whole site with a keyboard**, with visible focus states?
15. **Do all images have descriptive alt text** and all inputs have labels?

## What to do next

Fix the items that affect your highest-traffic pages first — usually the homepage, top service pages and contact page. Then measure the impact in GA4 before moving on.
