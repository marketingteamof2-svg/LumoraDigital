Content pillars are the three to five themes every piece of your content ladders up to. Done well, they make planning faster, keep your brand recognisable and ensure every post moves someone closer to buying.

## Why most content pillars fail

Most pillar lists look like this: "Educational, Inspirational, Promotional, Behind the scenes." Those aren't pillars — they're formats. They say nothing about *what* you'll talk about or *why your audience should care*.

## Step 1: Map your customer's journey

Write down what a customer needs to believe at each stage:

- **Before they know you:** "This problem is costing me more than I thought."
- **While comparing:** "This approach is better than the alternatives."
- **Before buying:** "This team can deliver for someone like me."

## Step 2: Find the overlap

Your best pillars sit where three things meet: what your audience needs to hear, what you have genuine expertise in, and what competitors aren't saying well.

## Step 3: Name them specifically

Instead of "Educational", try "Fixing D2C retention" or "Onboarding that converts". Specific names make it obvious what belongs and what doesn't.

## Step 4: Assign formats and goals

For each pillar, decide which formats suit it and which metric it should move. A "proof" pillar might be measured by profile visits and enquiries; an "awareness" pillar by shares and reach.

## Step 5: Review quarterly

Pillars aren't permanent. Every quarter, look at which ones drive the most engaged audience and enquiries, and adjust.
